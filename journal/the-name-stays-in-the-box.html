The name stays in the box

The unboxing is allowed to feel like an event. The thing you wear is not.

There is a way of opening a box that most clothes have forgotten. You know it from the best objects: the lid has weight, the thing inside is seated, nothing rattles, the first minute is quiet on purpose. Get Your Black borrows that ceremony and refuses the white.

The box is matte black, rigid, square enough to feel considered. On the lid, and only on the lid, the name is foiled: Get Your Black, and beneath it 得其黑. That is the entire public appearance of the house. Not on the cap. Not on a tag you have to cut off in a cab. On the lid.

You should not wear the box. The branding is a private object.

Open it and the sequence is simple. Tissue, folded rather than stuffed. A black dust pouch for the days the cap stays in. A card, plain. Then the cap, unmarked, the sand panel just visible, the buckle carrying no letters. The feeling we want is the one people describe as the care taken with a well-made object, which really means: someone decided the order of your hands.

You can leave the box on a stone table and it will look like it belongs there. Once the cap is on, the house goes silent again. Watch the short film if you want the order without the essay. It is silent, on purpose.

This is the whole idea of a silent brand. The people who will care about this cap are often the people who are tired of advertising someone else with their own head. They still want the object to be serious. They still want the unboxing to feel like an event. They simply do not want that event walking around with them in a restaurant.

So the name stays in the box. The cap goes out. If we have done it properly, you will not be able to tell who made it from across the room. You will only be able to tell that it was made.

The edition is 250. The price is HK$880. This site will not sell it to you. It will only show you the lid, and then the quiet thing inside.
