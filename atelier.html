The house · Hong Kong

A brand that refuses the head.

Get Your Black makes one thing: a serious black cap for people who do not like a name above their face. The city already has enough signals. This one stays in the wrapping.

Silent

No logo on the cloth. No logo on the buckle. If it can be seen from across a table, it does not belong on the cap.

Black only

There is no colourway. The sand panel is lining, not a second product. The house does not offer navy, and it does not offer white.

Boxed

The wordmark and the seal live on the lid, the card, and the pouch. You meet the brand when you open it. You leave it when you go out.

The seal

A cap, reduced to a line.

The mark is a crown and a brim, drawn as one gold seal. It is the house, not a badge for the street. You will find it on the box and in this room. You will not find it stitched into the panels.

得其黑。黑而不宣。

Price

HK$880. Not a shop.

The cap is composed for people who will spend between five hundred and one thousand Hong Kong dollars on a hat, and would rather the city not be told. This edition is HK$880, numbered to 250.

Nothing here can be bought. There is no bag, no payment, and no waiting list pretending to be one. When the house is ready to place a cap in someone’s hands, it will not be through a checkout on this page.
