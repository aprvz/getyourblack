# Get Your Black

得其黑. A silent luxury house for Hong Kong.

One product: a six-layer black cap with no logo. The name is foiled on the box, and nowhere on the cap. The site is a viewing room. Nothing is for sale.

The edition is shown at **HK$880**, inside a range of HK$500 to HK$1,000.

## Pages

- Home, with the cap, the six layers, and the unboxing film
- The Cap, specification and photographs
- The Box, the silent unboxing film
- Atelier, the house and the seal
- Journal, three essays
