The box · 名字留在盒中

The name stays in the box.

A rigid matte-black box. Gold on the lid, and only on the lid. Tissue, a pouch, then a cap with nothing to declare. The feeling is the one people know from the best objects: the lid has weight, and the first minute is quiet on purpose.

Silent film · 35 seconds · captions available. The cap in the film carries no logo.

The order of the hands

Lid. Tissue. Cap.

You can leave the box on a stone table. You should not wear the box. The branding is a private object: for the person who receives it, for the shelf, for the moment before going out. Once the cap is on, the house goes silent again.
